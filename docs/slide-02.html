02

IN THE LOOP

You are a step in the work

Nothing moves unless you move it. Answer the question, make the call, start the next piece. The work runs at exactly your speed — and stops dead the moment you look away.

Your attention is the bottleneck — and it does not scale.

ON THE LOOP

You watch it and steer it

It runs without you. You read what it is doing, decide if it is heading the right way, and change what it was asked for. You step in because you want to — not because it stalled.

Your judgement is the input — not your availability.

Ralph does not make you faster at the work. It gets you out of the middle of it — and puts you above it.
