IN THE LOOP (INTERACTIVE CLAUDE)

You are a step in the work

Nothing moves unless you move it. Answer the question, make the call, start the next piece. The work runs at exactly your speed — and stops dead the moment you look away.

Your attention is the bottleneck — and it does not scale and fills up your context window.

ON THE LOOP (RALPH)

You watch it and steer it

It runs without you. You read what it is doing, decide if it is heading the right way, and change what it was asked for. You step in because you want to — with a closed loop.

You trust Ralph will iteratively get to the specs you pointed it to.

Ralph's job is not to make you faster at the work. It's to get you out of the middle of it — and puts you above it.
