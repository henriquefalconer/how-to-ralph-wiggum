Told to build, Ralph reads the specs and the plan before every pass. Between them they answer the only two questions it has: what am I making and what am I doing next.

THE SPECS

What the thing is

Your vision of the product, how it should look, how it should behave, what the rules are, what counts as correct.

What pins down your vision, the reason a hundred separate passes add up to one coherent thing.

THE PLAN

What order to build it in

That same product broken into pieces and put in order, each one small enough to finish in a single pass and clear enough to tell when it is.

It doubles as the scoreboard. Finished pieces get marked off.
