Looking at a product only makes sense when a product already exists. If you are coming up with something new, you never need it.

COPYING SOMETHING THAT EXISTS

Let Ralph go and look

Point it at the real thing and let it work through the product itself, writing down what it finds as it goes.

The specs and the plan come out the far end, and nobody had to write them.

COMING UP WITH SOMETHING NEW

Just talk it through with Claude

Describe what you want, argue about it, have a discussion with it, and have Claude write the specs and the plan out of that conversation.

Nothing to inspect. Nothing to copy. Your vision, pinned down well enough to hand over.
